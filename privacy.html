# Khayyati Privacy Policy **Last updated: October 11, 2026** Khayyati is an application for managing tailoring shop customers, orders, payments, and order images. ## Information Stored by the App Khayyati stores information entered by the user, such as customer details, order details, payment records, and order images. This information is stored locally on the user's device. ## Google Drive Backups If the user chooses to use the Google Drive backup feature, the app requests Google account authorization to upload or download backup files. The user can choose the Google account used for this operation. Backup files may contain customer details, order information, payment records, and order images. These files are stored in the user's Google Drive. The user is responsible for managing access to those files. ## Data Sharing Khayyati does not operate its own server to collect customer records. Information is not intentionally shared with the app developer through the app's backup feature. Google services are subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms. ## Data Retention and Deletion Local data remains on the user's device until the user deletes it or removes the app and its stored data. Backup files in Google Drive can be managed or deleted by the user through Google Drive. ## Security Users should protect their device and Google account and avoid sharing backup files with unauthorized people. ## Children's Privacy Khayyati is a business management application and is not specifically designed for children. ## Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. ## Contact For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: kerodemo8@gmail.com
